— THE STUDIO
I like spaces to feel modern, but lived in. Thoughtfully designed around how people live, shaped by purpose and human empathy.

M. Bharath

FOUNDER, PRINCIPAL ARCHITECT & CREATIVE DIRECTOR

Trained as an architect in India and an urban planner, M. Bharath’s design sensibility is rooted in functional clarity, craft, and human empathy. After completing his Bachelor of Architecture (B.Arch) from JNAFAU in 2013 and his Master’s in Urban and Regional Planning (M.Tech – M.U.R.P) in 2015, he trained under Ar. Kiron Cheerla before establishing bhahaus as an independent multidisciplinary practice.

Since its inception in 2014, the studio has completed over fifty projects across private residences, boutique commercial, hospitality and interior architecture, with a particular focus on considered, enduring spaces. Operating across Hyderabad and Vijayawada, an integrated team of architects, interior designers, and project builders carries every project seamlessly from first sketch to final nail.

An active member of the Indian Institute of Architects (IIA) and registered with the Council of Architecture (COA), he balances spatial proportion, honest materiality, and passive climate responsiveness with how people actually live. That, and the constant exposure to craftsmanship, keeps the studio’s language contemporary in expression yet warm, layered, and deeply rooted in its context.

The studio name unites “BHA” (Bharath) and “HAUS” (inspired by Bauhaus functional clarity, craftsmanship, and integrated design thinking). The studio itself was designed the way we design for clients: plan first, then a short palette, then the detail. Soft neutrals, honed stone, warm wood floors and layered texture, with spatial clarity at the centre of it, lit gently and made for exploration.

Stone, timber, lime plaster and textiles are kept where they can be seen in daylight, because a palette decided on a screen rarely survives contact with a real room.
