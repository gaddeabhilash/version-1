STUDIO ETHOS & ORIGIN

Rooted in functional clarity, craft, and human empathy.

The Name Genesis: The name bhahaus originates from “BHA,” derived from founder Bharath, and “HAUS,” inspired by the iconic Bauhaus movement, which influenced the studio’s philosophy of functional clarity, craftsmanship, and integrated design thinking.

Our multidisciplinary firm has architects, designers, and builders bringing an integrated vision to every project — ensuring seamless continuity from the first sketch to the final nail.

We believe that good design is never merely visual; it shapes how people feel, interact, and dwell within spaces. Whether designing a 15,000 sq ft private estate or a boutique hospitality interior, every proportion, axis, and material transition is crafted with conscious deliberation.

CORE PHILOSOPHY

How we approach space.

01

Designing With Empathy

Every project begins by listening to the real human rhythms of the occupants. Spaces are calibrated to evoke calm, belong to their setting, and deepen emotional connection over time.

02

Bauhaus Functional Clarity

Honoring the Bauhaus tradition, we eliminate unnecessary ornamentation. Form strictly follows purpose, celebrated through honest materiality: microtopping, honed stone, lime plasters, and timber.

03

First Sketch to Final Nail

From architectural massing and municipal clearances to interior joinery and on-site project management, our architects and builders ensure uncompromised precision at every stage.

ABOUT THE FOUNDER

M. Bharath

M. Bharath is the Founder and Principal Architect of bhahaus, an architecture and design studio based in Hyderabad and Vijayawada. Rooted in empathy and human-centered design, bhahaus works across Architecture, Interior Design, Renovation, Urban Planning, and Project Execution.

Bharath completed his Bachelor of Architecture (B.Arch) from Jawaharlal Nehru Architecture and Fine Arts University in 2013 and his Master’s in Urban and Regional Planning (M.Tech – M.U.R.P) in 2015. Since 2014, he has been actively involved in architectural practice, trained under Ar. Kiron Cheerla and collaborated with multidisciplinary firms and professionals.

In 2017, he co-founded Studio Seven Consultants, which later evolved into the independent identity of bhahaus. Today, the studio continues to create thoughtful, timeless, and emotionally connected spaces — with empathy remaining the true soul of its design philosophy.
